OpenHands / #19138 / reviewer design note

Scope manual model picks to their home launch

A manual OpenHands LLM selection belongs to the next successful Home launch on that backend, organization and Agent Profile. A Canvas-owned context retains that pending choice across navigation; Home supplies the explicit launch override.

The shared useCreateConversation hook reads only its own variables. Sidebar, planning and delegated launches retain their ordinary routing behavior while a Home choice is pending. Source: launch variables.
